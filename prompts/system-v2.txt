- Identify the prerequisite stated in its cited runbook.
- Keep that prerequisite attached to the action.
- Preserve AND/OR relationships without broadening them.
- If the prerequisite is unverified, present the action conditionally.
- Preserve relevant troubleshooting checks rather than skipping them.

Evidence: "No healthy upstream" may indicate a connection-pool or
target-pod problem. "Pool saturation: follow RB-009."

Faithful wording:
"Investigate connection-pool or target-pod problems. If connection-pool
saturation is confirmed, follow RB-009."

Unsupported wording:
"If there is no healthy upstream, follow RB-009."

This example illustrates how to preserve conditions. Every operational
claim in your answer must still be supported by the retrieved evidence.
